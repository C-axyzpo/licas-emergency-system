LICAS Privacy Policy

Localized Intelligent Crisis Alert System

1. Introduction

LICAS (Localized Intelligent Crisis Alert System) is an emergency alarm and response system designed to support emergency reporting, monitoring, and notification within participating school environments.

This Privacy Policy explains what information LICAS may collect, how that information is used, and how it is handled when a user interacts with the LICAS system, including its Messenger-based emergency bot.

LICAS is designed as a school-based emergency support system. It supplements established emergency procedures and does not replace certified emergency services or official emergency communication channels.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how LICAS is used, the system may collect the following information:

  • Full name provided during student registration.
  • Student ID provided during registration.
  • Grade and section information.
  • Messenger account identifier (PSID) required to communicate with the LICAS Messenger bot.
  • Emergency reports submitted through the system.
  • Emergency type, location, and description provided in a report.
  • Notification delivery and system status information.
  • Information generated by LICAS monitoring units, such as temperature, humidity, vibration, smoke-level data when available, and human-presence readings.
  • Date and time associated with reports, alerts, sensor readings, and system events.

3. Messenger Information

When a student interacts with the LICAS Messenger bot, LICAS may receive information necessary to identify the Messenger conversation and respond to the user.

This may include the user's Messenger Page-Scoped User ID (PSID), message content sent to the bot, and information voluntarily provided by the user during registration or emergency reporting.

The Messenger identifier is used to associate the user's Messenger conversation with their LICAS registration and to deliver LICAS emergency notifications when applicable.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected by LICAS may be used for the following purposes:

  • Registering students for LICAS emergency notifications.
  • Receiving and recording emergency reports.
  • Identifying the source of an emergency report when the user has registered.
  • Sending emergency notifications through Messenger.
  • Displaying emergency reports and system events on the LICAS administrative dashboard.
  • Monitoring the operational status of LICAS monitoring units.
  • Maintaining system records and notification history.
  • Improving the reliability and functionality of the LICAS system.

5. Emergency Reports

Emergency reports submitted through LICAS may be made available to authorized system administrators or personnel responsible for emergency response and monitoring.

Reports may contain information such as the reported emergency type, location, description, reporter identity, and time of submission.

Users should avoid including unnecessary sensitive or private information in emergency reports.

6. Emergency Notifications

Students who register with LICAS may receive emergency notifications through the LICAS Messenger bot.

Notifications may include information such as the nature of an emergency, affected location, safety instructions, and other information considered necessary for emergency communication.

Authorized administrators may send notifications to all registered students or to selected registered students through the LICAS administrative dashboard.

7. Data Storage

LICAS uses a database infrastructure to store information required for system operation, including student registrations, emergency reports, notifications, alerts, monitoring-unit information, and sensor readings.

Access to administrative information is intended to be restricted through appropriate application and database controls.

8. Data Sharing

LICAS does not use collected information for advertising or commercial profiling.

Information may be processed by technology providers required to operate the system, including database, hosting, and messaging infrastructure.

Information may also be disclosed to authorized school or emergency-response personnel when reasonably necessary to respond to an emergency or operate the system.

9. Meta and Messenger

LICAS uses Meta's Messenger platform to provide its emergency communication functionality.

When interacting with the LICAS Messenger bot, users are also subject to the applicable policies and terms of Meta's services.

LICAS only requests and processes information necessary for its Messenger-based functionality.

10. Data Retention

LICAS may retain registration records, emergency reports, notifications, sensor records, and system logs for as long as reasonably necessary for system operation, emergency documentation, research, evaluation, or administrative purposes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the operational requirements of the system.

11. User Requests

Users may request information about the personal information associated with their LICAS registration or request correction of inaccurate information, subject to applicable requirements and verification.

Requests may be directed to the LICAS project administrator or the appropriate school authority responsible for the system.

12. Children's and Student Data

LICAS may be used by students in a school environment. Student information is therefore handled for the limited purposes of registration, emergency reporting, communication, and system administration.

Users should provide only information necessary for the intended LICAS function.

13. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information handled by LICAS.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through the system.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when LICAS functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be made available through the LICAS website.

15. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by LICAS, please contact the LICAS project administrator or the appropriate school authority responsible for the project.
